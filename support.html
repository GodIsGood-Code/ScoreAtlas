ScoreAtlas

Support

ScoreAtlas shows your sheet music on iPad and turns the pages by listening to you play. Inside the app, tap Piece and then Help for a full guide.

Common questions

The app doesn't turn pages when I play.

Each piece needs either its sheet music (a MusicXML file) or one taught playthrough. Tap Piece to add either. Then tap Follow and play.

The microphone doesn't work.

Open the iPad's Settings, find ScoreAtlas (or Safari, if you use the website), and allow the microphone. Then try Piece → Check microphone: the note names should light up as you play.

Pages turn too early or too late for me.

Open Settings in the app and move Turn pages earlier or later.

Where do I get a MusicXML file?

Many pieces are on MuseScore.com (download as MusicXML). For a scanned PDF, the free Audiveris app on a Mac or PC can read the scan and export MusicXML (.mxl).

Does my music leave my iPad?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
