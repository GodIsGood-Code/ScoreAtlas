    Help

    Getting started
    1. Tap the piece's name at the top left to open your Library, then Add music: a PDF (scans and photos of music work), with its sheet music file if you have one.
    2. Tap Piece and either Add sheet music (a MusicXML file) or Teach it.
    3. Tap Follow and play. The first time, allow the microphone.
    4. To try it straight away: the sample piece, Warm-ups, already has its sheet music. Open it from the Library, tap Follow and play from any page.
    Sheet music (MusicXML)

    With the piece's sheet music, ScoreAtlas follows it straight away, with no teaching, and highlights the bar you're playing.

    • From MuseScore.com: download the piece as MusicXML.
    • From your own scan: open the PDF in the free Audiveris app on a Mac or PC, let it read the music, and export MusicXML (.mxl).
    • Easiest, with a Mac: the ScoreAtlas Reader helper (download it from godisgood-code.github.io/ScoreAtlas/mac/ScoreAtlas-Reader.zip on the Mac, unzip, double-click the installer once). Drop PDFs onto it; each PDF and its sheet music appear in the ScoreAtlas folder in iCloud Drive.
    • On the iPad: Library → Add music, pick the PDF and its .mxl together (several pieces at once is fine). Or, for the open piece, Piece → Add sheet music.
    • Sheet music options: tick only the parts you play (a choir or orchestra part is left out), type the page the music starts on (one movement of a longer PDF), or the first bar of each page if the file came from a different edition.
    Teaching a piece

    Piece → Teach, then play the piece (or any section) while turning pages yourself with the pedal or a tap. Tap Done at the top when finished. Teach more sections any time; they add up. Forget clears what a piece learned.

    Following and practicing
    • Start anywhere, stop, repeat, go back or skip ahead: ScoreAtlas finds you again. After skipping, turn to that page yourself and it finds you within a second.
    • Everything for practicing is in the Practice menu at the top.
    • Loop a passage: then tap the first and the last bar. Each repetition is counted with its tempo.
    • Trouble spots: the passages where you hesitated, slowed down or missed notes lately. Tap one: it loops with the metronome a little slower, and each clean time steps the tempo up.
    • Hands: Left or Right, and Follow listens for that hand only (and Hear the music plays it).
    • Hear the music: plays the sheet music from the page on screen with the bar highlighted; drag Speed to slow it down.
    • Keep this take (in the practice report, or while listening back) saves the session's recording with the piece; Kept takes plays them back with the highlight, or shares them as sound files.
    • Metronome: tap the number to type a tempo, or Tap tempo; pick any time signature and 1–4 clicks a beat. With a loop, the tempo ladder speeds up after each clean time.
    • Tap Stop for the practice report: bars you practiced most, where you hesitated, your tempo bar by bar. Listen back plays your session with the pages following.
    • Check my notes (in the report, with sheet music): listens to the session for missed and wrong notes and marks those bars in red. It takes a little while and works without Wi-Fi. Not in my music stops a note from being checked (for notes the sheet music reader added by mistake). The Practice journal shows your notes over time.
    Library and setlists

    Every PDF you open is kept in the Library. In Setlists, make a list, add pieces in order and tap Play: at the end of a piece, the forward pedal opens the next one, and Follow carries on. Share sends a setlist with its music as one file; on the other iPad, Library → Add music opens it.

    Piece → Edit pages: take out blank pages, change the order, turn sideways scans, or add pages from another PDF. Markings move with their pages.

    Apple Pencil

    Tap Mark first (so nothing is marked by accident), then write with the Pencil or a finger. Tools: Pen (black, red, blue, or any color from the wheel), Highlight, Text (tap where the note goes, then type), Stamps (dynamics, hairpins, accents, fermata, breath and pedal marks), fingering 1–5 and the Eraser. The slider sets the size. Tap Done when finished. Markings stay with the piece.

    View, pedal and performing
    • View: two pages side by side when the iPad is sideways; half-page turns; Lock for performing (only the pedal turns pages; hold the lock button to unlock).
    • A Bluetooth page-turn pedal works as forward and back. Tap the right side of the page to go forward, the left side to go back, or swipe.
    • Pinch to zoom with two fingers; while zoomed in, one finger moves the page around (taps still turn pages). Whole page at the bottom right goes back.
    Offline and the Home Screen

    After loading once with Wi-Fi, ScoreAtlas works without it. For an app icon: Share → Add to Home Screen.

    Something isn't working
    • No pages turn: the piece needs sheet music or one taught playthrough (see Piece).
    • The microphone is off: in Safari, tap aA → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow, then reload. Check the microphone and the pedal (below) shows the notes it hears.
    • The microphone is off: open the iPad's Settings → ScoreAtlas and turn on Microphone. Check the microphone and the pedal (below) shows the notes it hears.
    • Turns feel late or early: Settings → Turn pages.
    • It loses you in a passage: turn to the page you're playing (it then stays there), or teach just that passage (Piece → Teach); taught sections are used where they fit better.
    • Check my notes flags a note you played: very soft notes can go unheard, and the sheet music reader sometimes misreads a note. If the same note is flagged every time, the reading is probably wrong there.

    Welcome to ScoreAtlas

    Your sheet music on iPad, with pages that turn by themselves while you play.

    Bring your music

    Tap the piece's name at the top left to open your Library, then Add music. Scans and photos of music work; add the sheet music file with it if you have one.

    Teach it, or add the sheet music

    Under Piece: add the piece's MusicXML file and ScoreAtlas follows it right away, or Teach it by playing it once while turning the pages yourself.

    Tap Follow and play

    The bar you're playing lights up, and the page turns just before you need it. Stop, repeat, skip around: it keeps up. Try it now on the sample piece, Warm-ups: its sheet music is ready.

    Practice with purpose

    The Practice menu: loop a passage, work on your trouble spots, practice hands separately, hear the music, use the metronome. Write fingerings and markings with Apple Pencil (tap Mark first).

    Ready for the stage

    Setlists, two pages side by side, half-page turns and a lock for performing. Everything stays on your iPad and works offline.

    When you first tap Follow, allow the microphone.

    1. Try it right away: the sample piece, Warm-ups, is in your Library with its sheet music. Open it, tap Follow and play.
    2. Practice → History: your last session, listening back, kept takes and the journal, in one place.

    From version 0.5.2:

    1. Pinch to zoom: two fingers on the music zoom in; one finger moves the page around; Whole page (bottom right) goes back. Pages turn as usual and show from their top.
    2. Keep this take: after a session, keep its recording with the piece (in the practice report, or while listening back). Practice → Kept takes: play them with the bar highlighted, rename, share or delete.
    3. The typefaces are now built in (no internet needed), ready for the App Store version.

    From version 0.5.1:

    1. Practice menu (top bar): loops, trouble spots, hands, hearing the music, the metronome, your report and journal, all in one place.
    2. Trouble spots: the bars you stumble on, looped one at a time with the metronome, faster each clean time.
    3. Hands separately: Follow listens for just the left or right hand.
    4. Hear the music: the sheet music played from this page, at any speed, with the bar highlighted.
    5. Mark: any pen size (slider), any color (color wheel), highlighter colors, stamps (dynamics, accents, fermata, breath and pedal marks) and text notes.
    6. Metronome: type the tempo, any time signature, 2–4 clicks a beat, tap tempo.

    8. Add music (Library): a PDF and its sheet music together, several at once. A Mac helper makes the sheet music for you (see Help).
    9. Share a setlist with its music; notes over time in the journal; Not in my music in Check my notes.
