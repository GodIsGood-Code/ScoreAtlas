ScoreAtlas

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ScoreAtlas is made by Ethan Chen.

In short: ScoreAtlas collects nothing. Your music, recordings and markings stay on your iPad.

What the app keeps, and where

ScoreAtlas stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • the PDFs you open (your library) and your setlists;
  • sheet music files you add (MusicXML) and what the app learns when you teach it a piece;
  • your Apple Pencil markings;
  • summaries of your practice sessions (which bars you played, tempo, hesitations);
  • your settings.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting a piece from the library deletes what the app kept about it; deleting the app deletes everything.

The microphone

ScoreAtlas uses the microphone only while you have Follow or Teach turned on (or the microphone check), to follow your playing and turn the pages. The sound is analyzed on your iPad as you play. The most recent session is kept in the device's memory so you can listen back to it or check your notes (the note recognition runs on your iPad too), and is discarded when you start a new session or close the app, unless you tap Keep this take: then it is saved on your iPad, in the app's own storage, until you delete it. Recordings are never sent to a server, and leave your iPad only when you share one yourself.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no sign-in. ScoreAtlas contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not collect, sell or share personal information of any kind.

Network use

The app works without an internet connection. On the website, when online, it may download its own updated files from this site. These requests contain no information about you or your music. The App Store app has everything built in and makes no requests at all.

Children

ScoreAtlas is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
