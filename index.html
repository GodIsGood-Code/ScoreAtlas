Test 8: steadier following, your own bar numbers, solo part

  1. Run-through first: open your piece, add its sheet music (or use what you added in Test 7), tap Follow and play it through. Repeated passages should stay on the page you're on, turns should come earlier, and the yellow highlight should move steadily.
  2. Then practice moves: stop and restart, go back a few bars, skip ahead, play a bit of something else.
  3. Sheet music from elsewhere (MuseScore, say): tap Add sheet music, pick the file, then under Sheet music options type the first bar number of each page of your PDF, starting with page 2, and tap Apply.
  4. Pieces with orchestra or other players: under Parts I play, keep only your part ticked and tap Apply.
