Practice report

Test 9: practice report, loops, steadier following

  1. Practice as usual with Follow, then tap Stop. A practice report opens: the bars you practised most, where you hesitated, and your tempo bar by bar (tap the graph for numbers). While it's open, the bars on the page are tinted by how often you played them.
  2. Loop a passage: tap Loop, then tap the first and the last bar on the page. Play it as often as you like with Follow on: the top bar counts the times and shows each one's tempo. When you stop at the end, the page goes back to the start of the loop. Tap Loop off to finish.
  3. Turns now come about half a second earlier, and bars are found properly on phone scans.
  4. After skipping somewhere, flip to that page with the pedal: the app then finds you within a second or so.
  5. Move a panel out of the way by dragging its title (the small bar at the top).
