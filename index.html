Test 4: practice mode

  1. Open a piece you've taught. To teach a new one: tap Teach, wait a second, play it from the beginning turning pages with your pedal, then tap Done.
  2. Flip to any page, tap Follow, wait a second, and start playing anywhere on that page.
  3. Practice as you normally would: stop, repeat a few bars, go back, skip ahead. The pages should keep up.
  4. Play something that isn't in the piece. Nothing should turn.

If you jump to another page, flipping there with your pedal helps it find you right away.
