Test 11: library, setlists, Pencil, display, offline

  1. Library: every PDF you open is kept there. Tap Library to open a piece, rename it or remove it. Add a PDF is there too.
  2. Setlists: in the Library, tap Setlists, New setlist, name it, add pieces and put them in order, then Play. On a piece's last page, the forward pedal opens the next piece (and Follow carries on if it was on).
  3. Apple Pencil: just write on the music. Tap ✎ for colours, the highlighter, fingering numbers (1–5), the eraser and Undo; while ✎ is on, a finger draws too. Markings are saved with the piece.
  4. View: two pages side by side when the iPad is sideways, half-page turns, and Lock for performing (only the pedal turns pages; hold the lock button to unlock).
  5. Offline: once loaded, the app works without Wi-Fi. For an app icon: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
